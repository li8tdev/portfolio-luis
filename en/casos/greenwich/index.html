[Case study] Websites

Greenwich Peninsula FC

Website · Football club · London, UK · greenwichpeninsulafc.com

Club website with news, per-category programmes and the week's results up to date. The coaching staff publish on their own: updates, squads and fixtures without depending on anyone.

View live site → See it in the portfolio
Challenge
Club information was spread across social media and PDFs: families could not find programmes or schedules.
Approach
A site of its own with a fixed structure (club, programmes, news) and a panel where the club publishes without touching code.
Delivery
Website published with news, programmes and results; the club started the season with everything in one place.
